Talk to any document

Give it a document or a web address, press Process, then talk to an AI voice agent that knows only that. It is an AI assistant powered by Google Gemini and, like any AI, it can make mistakes.

or

Your document and your conversation are sent to Google Gemini to power the conversation, so please do not upload anything private or sensitive.

This demo runs on a free Gemini key, so it may be busy (about three conversations at once) or used up for the day. Each visitor can talk for 5 minutes a day on the demo key, unless they add their own key in Settings. Documents are limited to about 32,000 tokens; longer ones are cut.
