Privacy: nothing you give is stored by this site or on our servers. Your document and your conversation are sent only to Google Gemini, to power the conversation; on a free key Google may use them to improve its products, so do not upload anything private.

This demo runs on a free Gemini key, so it may be busy (about three conversations at once) or used up for the day. Each visitor can talk for 5 minutes a day on the demo key, unless they add their own. Documents are limited to about 32,000 tokens; longer ones are cut.
